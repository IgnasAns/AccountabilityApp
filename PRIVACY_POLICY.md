# Privacy Policy for Do It Mate!

**Last updated: January 4, 2026**

Do It Mate! ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect the following types of information:
*   **Account Information**: When you sign up, we collect your email address, display name, and profile picture (optional) to create your account.
*   **User Content**: We collect the photos and creating data you upload as proof for your accountability goals ("Bets").
*   **Usage Data**: We may collect anonymous data about how you use the app to improve our services.
*   **Device Information**: We collect information about your device (model, OS version) to ensure app compatibility and troubleshoot issues.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
*   Provide and maintain the Service.
*   Verify your goal completions through our AI verification system and peer review.
*   Facilitate social interactions within your accountability groups.
*   Send you notifications about your goals and group activity.

## 3. Information Sharing
*   **Group Members**: Your display name, profile picture, goal progress, and photo proofs are visible to other members of the accountability groups you join.
*   **Service Providers**: We use third-party services (like Supabase for database/auth and Google Cloud for AI) to operate the app. These providers have access to your data only to perform tasks on our behalf.
*   **We do NOT sell your personal data to advertisers.**

## 4. Data Retention
We retain your personal information and content for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time within the app settings.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@doitmate.app.
