## Full Description
Do It Mate! is the ultimate social accountability app designed to help you and your friends achieve your goals—or pay the price if you don't.

**Key Features:**
*   **Create Accountability Groups**: key up with friends, family, or colleagues for fitness, learning, or any habit you want to build.
*   **Set Real Stakes**: Define the penalty for missing a check-in. Money adds a real incentive to stick to your word.
*   **Daily Check-ins**: Upload photo proof of your activity.
*   **AI & Peer Verification**: Our smart system (and your friends) verify your proof.
*   **Track Your Progress**: Visual graphs and stats show your consistency over time.

Don't just say you'll do it. Do It, Mate!
